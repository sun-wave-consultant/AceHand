Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 22, 2026

AceHand is provided by Sun Wave Consultant LLC. For privacy-related inquiries, please contact support@sun-wave.xyz .

AceHand is an entertainment poker game using virtual chips. The app does not offer deposits, chip purchases, cash withdrawals, or chip redemption. Virtual chips have no real-world monetary value.

1. Local Data

AceHand stores game preferences, up to 50 recent poker hands, and limited diagnostic records locally on your device.

This information is used to restore settings, review previous hands, and troubleshoot technical issues. These records are not automatically uploaded to servers operated by us.

Device backups may depend on your Apple and system backup settings. AceHand does not provide its own cloud synchronization service.

2. Game Center and Multiplayer

AceHand uses Apple Game Center for multiplayer gameplay and friend invitations.

When necessary for functionality and permitted by applicable permissions, the app may access Game Center player identifiers, display names, avatars, friends, and recently played players.

This information is used to identify player seats, display player information, and send multiplayer invitations.

Player information and game state necessary for multiplayer sessions are transmitted between participating devices through Game Center.

Other participants may see player details and public game information displayed during gameplay.

AceHand does not provide your Apple Account password to other players.

Apple's processing of information through Game Center is governed by Apple's own privacy policy and applicable service terms. You can manage your Game Center account and relevant permissions through your device settings.

Single-player mode does not require Game Center sign-in.

3. Diagnostics and Support

AceHand may store limited troubleshooting information locally, including application version, device model, operating system version, connection status, and event timestamps.

Diagnostic logs are not automatically transmitted to us. Recipients receive diagnostic information only when you voluntarily choose to share or send it.

If you contact customer support, we may process your reply email address, issue description, and any attachments you voluntarily provide.

This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and resolve the reported issue. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or unrelated personal information.

Support communications are retained for up to 12 months after the last interaction, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable law. You may request earlier deletion by contacting us.

4. Advertising, Tracking, and Sharing

The current version of AceHand does not integrate advertising SDKs, third-party behavioral analytics SDKs, or advertising tracking SDKs.

We do not sell personal data or use the information described in this policy for cross-app advertising tracking.

Apart from providing Game Center multiplayer functionality, processing support requests initiated by you, and disclosures required by applicable law, we do not disclose your personal information to other organizations.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Recent poker hand records are automatically replaced when the local storage limit is reached. Diagnostic records are maintained in bounded local storage.

Deleting AceHand normally removes its local application data from your device.

Information retained in system backups or Apple services must be managed through the relevant services and settings.

Deleting AceHand does not automatically delete game records already stored on other multiplayer participants' devices.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You may choose not to grant friend-related permissions or not to use multiplayer features. You may stop providing support information at any time.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of support information we have received, contact support@sun-wave.xyz .

Certain information may need to be retained where required by applicable law.

7. Age Eligibility

AceHand contains simulated gambling content and is not directed at children. Eligibility to use the app depends on applicable local requirements and its App Store age rating.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Material changes to how information is used will be explained, and consent will be obtained where required by applicable law.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 22 日

AceHand 由 Sun Wave Consultant LLC 提供。 隐私相关问题请联系： support@sun-wave.xyz 。

AceHand 是一款使用虚拟筹码的娱乐扑克游戏。 本应用不提供充值、筹码购买、现金提现或筹码兑换业务。 虚拟筹码没有现实货币价值。

1. 本地数据

AceHand 在你的设备上保存游戏偏好、 最近最多 50 手牌局记录以及有限的诊断记录。

这些信息用于恢复游戏设置、 查看历史牌局和排查技术故障。 这些记录不会自动上传到由我们运营的服务器。

设备备份可能受到 Apple 和系统备份设置的影响。 AceHand 不提供自己的云端同步服务。

2. Game Center 与多人游戏

AceHand 使用 Apple Game Center 提供多人联机和好友邀请功能。

在功能需要且相关权限允许的情况下， 应用可能访问 Game Center 玩家标识、 显示名称、头像、好友以及最近一起游戏的玩家信息。

这些信息用于识别玩家座位、 显示玩家资料和发送多人游戏邀请。

多人游戏所需的玩家信息和牌局状态 通过 Game Center 在参与设备之间传输。

其他参与者可能看到游戏中展示的玩家资料 及公开牌局信息。

AceHand 不会向其他玩家提供你的 Apple 账号密码。

Apple 对 Game Center 服务中数据的处理 受其自身隐私政策及适用服务条款约束。 你可以通过设备设置管理 Game Center 账号及相关权限。

单机模式不要求登录 Game Center。

3. 诊断与客服支持

AceHand 可能在本地保存有限的故障排查信息， 包括应用版本、设备型号、操作系统版本、 网络连接状态和事件时间戳。

诊断日志不会自动发送给我们。 只有当你主动分享或发送相关内容时， 接收方才会获得你选择提供的信息。

如果你联系客服，我们可能处理你的回复邮箱、 问题描述以及你主动提供的附件。

这些信息仅用于回应你的请求和解决相关问题。 请不要发送密码、支付信息或其他无关的个人资料。

客服沟通记录在最后一次互动后 最多保留 12 个月， 法律要求更长保留期限的情况除外。 你可以通过邮件请求提前删除。

4. 广告、追踪与信息共享

当前版本的 AceHand 未集成广告 SDK、 第三方行为分析 SDK 或广告追踪 SDK。

我们不会出售个人数据， 也不会将本政策描述的信息用于跨应用广告追踪。

除提供 Game Center 多人联机功能、 处理你主动提出的支持请求以及法律要求的披露外， 我们不会向其他机构披露你的个人信息。

5. 数据保留与删除

本地牌局记录达到数量上限后会自动滚动替换。 诊断记录保存在容量受限的本地存储中。

删除 AceHand 通常会移除设备上的应用本地数据。

系统备份及 Apple 服务中保留的数据， 需要通过相应服务及设置进行管理。

删除 AceHand 不会自动删除 其他多人游戏参与者设备上 已经保存的牌局记录。

6. 你的选择与权利

你可以选择不授予好友相关权限， 或不使用多人联机功能。 你也可以随时停止向客服提供信息。

对于我们已经收到的客服资料， 你可以发送邮件至 support@sun-wave.xyz 请求查阅、更正或删除。

法律要求保留的部分除外。

7. 年龄要求

AceHand 包含模拟赌博内容， 不面向儿童。 使用资格取决于当地适用规定 以及 App Store 的年龄分级。
