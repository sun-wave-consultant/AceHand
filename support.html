Frequently Asked Questions

1. How does single-player mode work?

You can play Texas Hold'em against computer opponents without signing in to Game Center.

2. How do I play with friends?

AceHand uses Apple Game Center for multiplayer gameplay.

Sign in to Game Center through your device settings and allow the permissions needed for multiplayer features.

All participants should use compatible versions of AceHand.

3. Why are multiplayer invitations not working?

If you cannot send or receive invitations, please check the following:

  • Both players are signed in to Game Center.
  • Both devices have a working internet connection.
  • Both players are using compatible app versions.
  • Device settings or system restrictions are not blocking multiplayer features.

4. Where is my hand history stored?

AceHand stores up to 50 recent poker hands locally on your device.

The app does not provide its own cloud synchronization service.

Deleting the app may remove your locally stored hand history.

5. Can I purchase or redeem chips?

No. AceHand uses virtual chips for entertainment only.

Virtual chips have no real-world monetary value. The app does not support deposits, chip purchases, cash withdrawals, or chip redemption.

6. How can I report a technical problem?

Please email our support team and include:

  • AceHand app version
  • iOS or iPadOS version
  • Device model, if available
  • Steps to reproduce the issue
  • Optional screenshots

You may also share diagnostic logs if you choose to do so. Logs are not automatically sent to us.

常见问题

1. 如何使用单机模式？

AceHand 支持与电脑玩家进行德州扑克对局。 单机模式无需登录 Game Center。

2. 如何与好友联机？

AceHand 使用 Apple Game Center 提供多人联机功能。

请在设备设置中登录 Game Center， 并允许使用多人游戏所需的相关权限。

所有参与者应安装兼容版本的 AceHand。

3. 无法发送或接收好友邀请怎么办？

如果好友邀请无法正常使用， 请检查以下项目：

  • 双方是否已经登录 Game Center。
  • 双方设备是否具有正常的网络连接。
  • 双方是否使用兼容的应用版本。
  • 设备设置或系统限制是否阻止了多人游戏功能。

4. 牌局记录保存在哪里？

AceHand 在本机保存最近最多 50 手牌局记录。

应用不提供自有的云端同步服务。 删除应用可能导致本地牌局记录丢失。

5. 可以购买或兑换筹码吗？

不可以。 AceHand 的虚拟筹码仅用于娱乐， 不具有现实货币价值。

游戏不支持充值、筹码购买、 现金提现或筹码兑换。

6. 如何报告技术问题？

请通过电子邮件联系客服， 并尽可能提供：

  • AceHand 应用版本
  • iOS 或 iPadOS 版本
  • 设备型号（如方便提供）
  • 问题发生的具体步骤
  • 可选的截图

如果你愿意，也可以主动分享诊断日志。 应用不会自动向我们发送这些日志。
